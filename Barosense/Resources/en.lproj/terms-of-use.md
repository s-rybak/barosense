## 6. Accuracy — what is not guaranteed

The app is provided as it is, and specifically:

- **Sensor readings.** Barosense reports what your iPhone's barometer gives it. That sensor
  has its own tolerance, it measures pressure where you are standing rather than sea-level
  pressure, and it moves when you change altitude. Readings are not calibrated
  instrumentation.
- **Forecasts.** Forward pressure values come from Apple Weather or from a curve fitted to
  your own readings. Both can be wrong, and neither is produced by us.
- **Notifications.** iOS decides whether and when a background task or a notification is
  delivered. Barosense cannot guarantee that any given notification arrives, arrives on
  time, or arrives at all. Do not rely on the app to alert you to anything that matters.
- **Continuity.** Readings arrive irregularly, with gaps. That is how the platform works and
  is expected behaviour, not a defect.

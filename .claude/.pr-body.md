## History screen, My medications, and a day picker on the medication sheet

Builds the History destination from the `M3 · Історія` frame and adds the medications page
behind a row under the calendar. Replaces `PlaceholderScreen` for `.history`. Also lets the
add-medication sheet file a dose on an earlier day, which it could not do before.

### What lands

- **Period picker** — Місяць / 3М / Рік / Все. Every period is a span of whole calendar
  months **ending with the month the grid is showing**, so the ‹ › arrows move the counts
  card and the grid together and the card can never describe a window the user is not
  looking at.
- **Counts card** — "Травень 2026 · 28 записів" over the two most-used tags and the number
  of medication entries in the window. Counts only; nothing ranks a period or compares it
  to another.
- **Month grid** — one cell per day, filled with that day's **peak** intensity. Averaging
  would hide the day the user opened the calendar to find: one 9 with two 2s averages to 4
  and draws green. Days with no check-in are left unfilled — "nothing recorded" and
  "recorded, and it was mild" are different facts.
- **My medications** — pushed from a row under the grid, scoped to the same window. Groups
  the user's own entries by name: the spelling they last used, the amounts they typed, how
  many times, and when last.

### "Other day" on the add-medication sheet

The "Time taken" row's middle chip was a clock reading — the last ten-minute mark before now,
`09:40` at 09:41. It has been replaced by an "Other day" chip that reveals a month calendar.

The mark bought the user nothing: it named a moment at most ten minutes before "Now", and
nothing this app measures resolves that finely. What was missing instead was the day. A time
wheel has no date, so a dose taken yesterday could only be entered as one taken today — filed
against the wrong day, colouring the wrong cell in the grid this same PR adds.

- The calendar appears on the first tap, not behind a second one, which is the promise
  "+ Other dose" and "Other time" already make.
- The picker edits the date components only and carries the time of day across, seeded from
  whatever was already selected. Choosing yesterday for a dose placed at 09:00 keeps 09:00.
- Nothing after today is selectable. Seeding from the current selection — which is never in
  the future — is what keeps that range honest.
- `DatePicker(.graphical)` rather than a hand-built grid. The objection that forced `TimeWheel`
  to be built from `Picker` was `.wheel`'s washed-out digits; `.graphical` takes its accent from
  `tint` and its numerals from the label colour, so a second month grid to keep in step with
  `HistoryCalendarCard` would buy nothing.
- The medication row on the check-in sheet now prints the day in front of the time when that day
  is not today. Without it a dose taken this morning and one taken last Tuesday draw the
  identical row, which makes the new chip unverifiable from the form.

### Swipe to delete, and an order for the medications list

- The "×" on a recorded medication row is gone; the row is swiped left to reveal a red trash
  action instead. `.swipeActions` is a `List` modifier and this box is a `VStack` inside the
  scaffold's `ScrollView`, so the gesture is hand-built — about thirty lines, against a `List`
  inside a `ScrollView` and a height the box would have to be told rather than take from its
  rows. One row is open at a time; a tap anywhere on an open row puts it back.
- White on the red rather than `Palette.onInk`: measured at 3.85:1 against 3.44:1. At 14 pt bold
  the label counts as large text, where WCAG AA asks 3:1 and both clear it — white keeps the
  margin. Neither reaches the 4.5:1 a caption-sized label needs, which is why the label is
  14 pt bold rather than a caption.
- Removing the button took away the only control VoiceOver could reach, so the row is now a
  single element carrying a "Delete" accessibility action. That is the whole affordance for a
  VoiceOver user, not a convenience on top of a visible button.
- "My medications" gained a Recent / A–Z picker, the same shared `SegmentedSelector` the other
  two screens use, shown only once there are two rows to order. Sorting lives in `Shared/` as
  `MedicationOrder` + `MedicationHistory.ordered(_:by:)`, separate from `summaries(in:)` so
  switching the order does not go back to the store — grouping is the expensive half and does
  not change when the order does.
- `localizedStandardCompare`, not `<`. Comparing `String` directly orders by Unicode scalar,
  which files every Cyrillic name after every Latin one and sorts "ібупрофен" away from
  "Ібупрофен". Pinned by a test in both alphabets. The sort is decorated with the incoming index
  because `sorted(by:)` is not documented as stable and the input already carries meaning.

### Divergences from the frame

1. **The link row is not in the design.** The medications page had to be reachable, and the
   counts card already puts a medication figure on this screen with nowhere to go from it.
   Styled as one of the app's existing choice rows rather than a system list row.
2. **The frame's calendar starts May 2026 on a Monday; 1 May 2026 is a Friday.** The grid
   computes its own offset from `Calendar.firstWeekday`, so it starts on Monday for a
   Ukrainian locale and on Sunday for a US one, with the day landing where it actually falls.

### Accessibility

The day cell's numeral is dark on every filled cell and never flips to light. Measured, not
assumed: the intensity ramp has no dark end, and the darkest fill a cell can take composites
to about `#D96858`, where `Palette.heading` gives ~4.7:1 and `Palette.onInk` ~3.7:1. At 13 pt
semibold the numeral is not "large text", so 3.7 fails WCAG AA where 4.7 passes. Fill opacity
carries the same value as hue, so the grid still reads in greyscale, and each recorded day
exposes its figures to VoiceOver rather than a colour name.

### Compliance

No medical claims: the forbidden-vocabulary sweep is clean across every new file. Neither
screen relates a medication to how the user felt, totals a daily amount, works out an
interval, or suggests a next dose. Both are read-only. No new HealthKit type, no network,
no new dependency.

### Scope

14 files, ~1 980 lines — over the 900-line soft limit. 436 of those are
`Localizable.xcstrings`: 20 new keys in a JSON catalog at ~23 lines each, no logic. Actual
source is ~1 100 lines across two new screens, one shared model and its tests.

`PressureChartCard`'s private range selector was replaced by a shared
`SegmentedSelector` rather than copied — both places draw the identical control, and the
second copy is where a design change starts landing on one screen and not the other.

### Verification

- `xcodebuild test` — **271 tests, 0 failures** (was 243; +28 covering windows, grid layout
  across first-weekday and leap-year cases, local-day bucketing, peak merging, tag ranking
  and its tie-break, medication grouping, and both summary orders in Latin and Cyrillic).
- Run on an iPhone 17 simulator in a Ukrainian locale: plural agreement checked at 1 and 2
  ("1 запис" / "2 записи", "1 раз"), Monday-first header, forward arrow correctly disabled
  in the current month and enabled after stepping back.
- The day picker exercised on the same run: calendar opens on the first tap of "Інший день",
  Monday-first with future days greyed out, and a dose placed on the 10th came back as
  "Ibuprofen · 400 mg · 10 серп., 0:06" while one left on "Зараз" stayed "Ibuprofen · 400 mg ·
  0:07" — the day added only where it carries information, and the time of day preserved
  across the day change.
- Swipe delete exercised on the same run: the action reveals with a trash icon and «Видалити»,
  opening a second row closes the first, and the tap deletes the row. A first attempt did
  nothing — `.contentShape(.rect)` applied after `.offset` re-drew the hit area over the row's
  original width, so the open row kept catching taps meant for the button. Fixed by ordering
  `.offset` last, and the reason is written next to it.
- Order picker: "Нещодавні" gave Zinc, Aspirin, Ibuprofen (recency) and "А–Я" gave Aspirin,
  Ibuprofen, Zinc.
- SwiftLint **not run** — still not installed on this machine. Line length, `Shared/`
  UI-freedom and the vocabulary list were checked by hand.

### Follow-ups (not in this change)

- `OnboardingStepScaffold`'s pinned action overlaps content at accessibility sizes — a
  `safeAreaInset(edge: .bottom)` fix touching seven screens.
- No check-in edit or delete UI; the grid cells are not tappable yet.
- A multi-month window title repeats the year ("Трав. 2026 – Лип. 2026"). Unambiguous
  across a year boundary, but a same-year branch would read better.
